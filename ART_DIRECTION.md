# 산호 수조 — art direction (before implementation)

A pocket-sized illustrated field journal submerged in a twilight lagoon. Fixed 390×700 portrait composition scales to 320px phones and centers as a deliberate card on desktop. Background: midnight blue #102538, teal #1C6271, warm cream #F4E9C9; accents coral #FF907A, lime #B9D986, glacial cyan #95DBD4. Draw bespoke layered seabed, branching coral, swaying weeds, fish with fins and eyes, bubbles, sunlight shafts and sparse stars as vector shapes in Godot, not external clip art. Threat tints the water and dims fish. The three action cards use a different motif (food flakes, air bubbles, cleaning shimmer), animated confirmation, large hit areas. Korean Nanum Gothic OFL bundled for every line; 16–36px base typography with robust contrast. Screens: illustrated title with prominent start; a three-step illustrated rule card; dashboard with large day number, tank, three legible gauges and action choices; full-screen success/failure editorial cards. All art and a short synthesized two-tone chime are generated locally; no stock assets. Motion is gentle and can be disabled with OS reduced motion in a future version. No real husbandry claims; values are an abstract game system.
